Alpha Video v2: the training data (MAGICK on gray, white and black, 768×768)

Part of Alpha Video v2. Every image below is a lossless PNG of exactly what the trainer encodes.

Adapter lineage

Stock H3
MiniMax H3, no adapter. With the gray suffix in the prompt it already draws most subjects on flat gray (entry 1 of the Alpha Video v2 page).
→
Gray magic v3 (Δ_graymagic_v3)
A LoRA on stock H3, trained on the gray images below with their captions. Since the captions already ask for gray, it mostly learns MAGICK's look and a flatter, darker gray (tasting: border level 122, i.e. 0.48, vs 144 from the words alone).
→
Split v3 (Δ_split_v3)
Trained with gray magic v3 baked in: a LoRA plus three output heads, taught the gray, white and black images of the same row at once. From the three, least squares gives the cut-out (RGBA).

How the images are made

Each row is a MAGICK image with a true alpha matte (1024×1024 RGBA). It is composited in 8 bit on 50% gray (128), on white and on black at 1024, then area-resized to 768×768 and encoded by H3's VAE as a single-frame latent (24 × 1 × 48 × 48). The caption is the subject-only caption written for H3 Magic (it never mentions a background) plus the suffix chosen in entry 1: "The subject is isolated on a plain flat medium-gray (#808080) background, like a cut-out on an empty gray canvas."
