SANA-Sprint in-context alpha → RGB on gray: journal

We teach SANA-Sprint to take an alpha matte and a caption and paint the object on a flat 50% gray background. One entry per step. Every step uses the same 29 eval images and the same noise.

A mini genetic frenzy of sampling decoders and their deterministic twins against H3's decoder on 68 held-out images, scored by PSNR (single and mean of 8 seeds), DISTS and LPIPS, with the fitness gates, 1:1 texture crops, every image at full resolution, the lineage of every candidate and the survey.
