SANA-Sprint in-context alpha → RGB on gray: journal

We teach SANA-Sprint to take an alpha matte and a caption and paint the object on a flat 50% gray background. One entry per step. Every step uses the same 29 eval images and the same noise.

  • Step 6: adapter_v2. The key finding: the adapter must not know the gray 2026-11-17. Subtracting adapter_v1 removed the gray backdrop (IoU 0.48). adapter_v2, trained on random solid colors, fixes it: full subtraction IoU 0.92 on the teacher, 0.96 on stock Sprint in 2 steps with no training. Results table, 13 items at full resolution, caveats, WebGPU demo link.
  • Step 0: curation 2026-11-17. MAGICK turned into a BlueJay table of mattes, gray composites and latents: 140,955 images, 17 failures, 29 held out for eval.
  • Step 1: baseline 2026-11-17. Stock Sprint and teacher on the eval prompts, with and without a gray-background suffix.
  • Step 2: adapter 2026-11-17. A full fine-tune of the teacher on MAGICK-on-gray. Checkpoint 1: flat gray backgrounds, no artifacts.
  • Steps 3 and 4: control and subtraction 2026-11-17. The control follows mattes (IoU 0.97). Subtracting the adapter loses the gray background; keeping 75% of the adapter fixes it. The fix, adapter_v2, is in step 6.
  • Step 5: transfer to Sprint 2026-11-17. The control moved onto the stock Sprint student with no training: works with a toy control, loses the gray with the real one.
  • WebGPU demo: try the one-step model in your browser 2026-11-17. Draw or upload a matte, add text and shapes, type a prompt, generate on your own GPU. Pick a model version; downloads are cached in the browser. Served by ./run_web.sh; webgpu/?version=NAME opens one version.
  • Gallery: control_v1 step 1500, with and without subtracting the adapter 2026-11-17. 67 items (29 held-out, 38 train), MAGICK caption and detailed prompt, stock Sprint/teacher, teacher λ sweep, Sprint λ sweep at 1 and 2 steps. Full-resolution images.
  • Gallery: control_v2 step 1500 — the final model works on Sprint in 1–2 steps 2026-11-17. Full subtraction keeps matte and gray: teacher IoU 0.92, Sprint 2-step 0.96, Sprint 1-step 0.83. Same 67 items and columns.
  • Gallery: control_v2 step 500 (adapter trained on random-color backgrounds), with and without subtracting the adapter 2026-11-17. Same 67 items and columns as v1. Full subtraction now keeps the gray background: IoU 0.84 (v1: 0.48).

Entries are written as work happens, so each says what was and was not tested. Numbers in the tables are read from the metrics.json files in assets/.

Published images are full-resolution WebP q95, hosted on the repo's journal-assets branch; originals are PNG in the repo's BlueJay database.
