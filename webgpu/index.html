How uploads and shapes work

Upload: a PNG with transparency uses its alpha, any other image its brightness. Text, rectangles, ellipses, polygons and lines stay movable: drag them, drag the corner square to resize, Delete removes the selected one; with Move, picking a paint value recolors the selected one. Filters flatten everything into one layer (Undo brings the shapes back).

About these settings

Sprint takes guidance as an input embedding: one transformer pass per step, not two-pass CFG. Angles s are TrigFlow timesteps (π/2 = pure noise). 2 steps run at smax then smid; 1, 3 and 4 steps are evenly spaced from smax to 0, as in diffusers' SCMScheduler.
