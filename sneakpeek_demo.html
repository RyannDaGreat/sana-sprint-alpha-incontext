Sneak peek: how the live preview evolves

FIBO2 splitter, training step 2,000 (with its trained sneak peek highway), on the turbo base: 4 steps, 1024², no CFG, the shipped mode. Each frame is one preview the web app can show: after each of the 44 transformer blocks of every step, the highway's estimate of the final image (Tweedie x̂0), blended with the previous step's estimate by the app's calibrated weight w; then each step's own estimate, the last being the image. Left: the shipped fast decoder (what the app shows, r5a3_long). Right: the full FLUX.2 decoder on the same latent (the reference). All images are the original 1024² PNGs, lossless, shown 1:1 (scroll sideways). The curve is the relative error of the shown latent against the final one; ● marks frames the app actually shows (it skips a preview expected to be worse than the one on screen).

Published images are the original PNG files (original resolution, lossless), hosted on the repo's journal-assets branch.
