FIBO2 training previews

Bria FIBO2 (Fibo 2.0 dev, 8.1B) trained the same way as our SANA trimask model: an adapter LoRA first, then a control LoRA that reads the trimask (white = object, gray = unknown, black = background) through FIBO2's native context input. Previews are written by the trainer every 500 steps: 4 fixed train items, fixed noise, 20 sampler steps, CFG 5, generated at 1024². They are training-time samples (adapter + control on, λ = 1), not the held-out evals. Every image here is the trainer's original PNG file, untouched.

Resolution: up to step 2000 the trainer saved its previews at ¼ size (256 px per cell), so those files are small; nothing better exists for them. From step 2500 on the trainer saves the full 1024 px grid (fixed in commit 612ee44). Full-resolution renders of the earlier checkpoints come from BlueJay evals.
