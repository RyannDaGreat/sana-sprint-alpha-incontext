Cross-step block reuse (training-free)

Todo 128, phase 1 (m00820). Can FIBO2 skip transformer blocks on later sampler steps by reusing what each block did on the step before? A skipped block returns its input plus the residual (output − input) it produced the last time it ran (FORA / Δ-DiT style). Model: splitter step 5000. Quality is the final gray image against the full run with the same seed (LPIPS ↓, SSIM ↑). Turbo: 4 steps, 16 held-out items. Dev: 20 steps with CFG 5, 8 items. “Skipped” counts block evaluations of the 44 main blocks; the refiner blocks, embedders and final layer always run.

Findings

  • Whole-step reuse wins; skipping some blocks of a step loses. At the same skip rate, reusing every block of one step beats skipping half the blocks, every other block, or the blocks that change least.
  • Turbo: reusing all 44 blocks at step 2 skips 25% of the block evaluations for LPIPS 0.014 (λ 0) / 0.016 (λ 1) against the full run. 50% (steps 2 and 3) costs 0.098 / 0.069: visible.
  • Dev: the middle steps barely change (residual change 6–8%, cosine ≈ 1.000). Running every 3rd step from step 10 on skips 35% for LPIPS 0.012 / 0.002; FORA3 skips 55% for 0.034 / 0.013.
  • Cheap in the app: reusing a whole step needs one cached tensor, not 44: the last block's output is the step's input plus the sum of all residuals, so out_s = out_{s−1} + (in_s − in_{s−1}).
  • Early blocks change least (blocks 0–14: residual change 0.29 vs 0.48 for 15–43 in turbo), and reusing one of them costs least (LPIPS 0.013 vs 0.027).
