H3 Magic: split v3-grid (gray, white / black, alpha in one 2×2 frame)

Part of Alpha Video v2. Every item is the unmodified output; the |Δα| heat maps are drawn from the two alphas.

The idea (m01003, m01005, m01006). Instead of three output heads, H3 draws one 768×768 frame made of four 384×384 panels: top left the subject on 50% gray, top right on white, bottom left on black, bottom right its alpha matte in grayscale (white = opaque). Because the four panels are one image, attention is shared and the subject should match across them. The three background panels give the colour (decontamination: solve for the foreground colour given the known backgrounds), and the 4th panel is the alpha (the mask). The alpha that the three backgrounds imply on their own (least squares) is a free check: how much the two disagree, |Δα|, is reported per image.

Training. One LoRA on H3 + gray magic v3t (gray magic v3 fine-tuned on tiled latents), trained on the 10,000 MAGICK images laid out as such grids (tiled VAE encode, manifest §25), guidance loss (scale 4), lr 1e-4. Caption = the subject-only caption + this sentence (also at the end of every test prompt, q01–q20): Shown as a 2x2 grid of the same image: top left on a plain flat medium-gray (#808080) background, top right on a plain white background, bottom left on a plain black background, bottom right its grayscale alpha matte, white subject on black.

The grid video LoRA (10:40 AM): the best video model of Alpha Video v2

Why. Trained on still images only, the grid sometimes loses its layout in video: the cat starts as one full-frame cat, the candle's white panel turns black, the steam train's panels drift. Fix: a small LoRA (split_grid_video) trained on video with gray magic v3t and the grid LoRA baked in: 300 synthetic clips in which a MAGICK cut-out moves along a smooth random path for 39 frames, rendered exactly as 2×2 grids (gray, white / black, alpha) and tiled-encoded (table h3_motion_clips). Guidance loss 4, lr 1e-4, 250 steps on node 2's GPUs 0–3 (~11 s per step); validation 1.91 → 1.45 → 1.38 → 1.37 at steps 1 / 50 / 100 / 150.

Colour. Colour is decontaminated with the panel alpha by the per-pixel median of the three foreground estimates F_k = (C_k − (1 − α)·b_k)/α (one inconsistent panel cannot wash the colour out).
