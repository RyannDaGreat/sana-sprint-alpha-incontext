Summary

The sneak peek shows a preview after every sampler step and every transformer block. A preview needs a fast decoder: latents → pixels far faster than the full FLUX.2 VAE decoder (here 207 ms per 1024² on an A100 with ONNX Runtime; 4,853 ms in the user's browser). The user's limit is a 10% slowdown, later also 20% (m00807, m00835): per preview 0.10 × 99,208 ms / (2 passes × 46 blocks + 2 steps) ≈ 105 ms in the browser, which is ≈ 4.5 ms on the A100 at the browser/A100 ratio of the full decoder (20%: ≈ 9.0 ms), including the 0.24 ms highway head.

188 candidates from a genetic search (rounds of 5 Sonnet agents, mutations and crossovers of the best) were all re-run on the same 300 held-out 1024² images (the full decoder: SSIM 0.985, ΔE2000 1.02 against the originals) and timed with the GPU to themselves.

Glossary
  • Sneak peek: previews during a generation, after every step and every transformer block.
  • Fast decoder: a small network latent (1, 32, LH, LW) → image (1, 3, 8·LH, 8·LW) in [0, 1], replacing the full VAE decoder for previews only.
  • Sneak peek highway: one small head per transformer block that predicts the final layer's output from that block, through a gradient stop.
  • Tweedie estimate x̂0: x_t − σ·v, the clean latent the model implies at a step; what a preview shows.
  • SSIM / LPIPS / ΔE2000 / PSNR: structural similarity (1 = identical), learned perceptual distance (alex; 0 = identical), CIEDE2000 color difference (mean over pixels, first 48 test rows), peak signal-to-noise ratio, each against the original image.
  • Clean-GPU timing: every other process on the GPU paused, ONNX Runtime CUDA, one 1024² decode, median of 25 after 5 warm-ups (m00806).
  • Round / track: one generation of the genetic search; the 10% and 20% budgets, each with its own winners.
  • λ: the adapter fraction; the decoders do not depend on it (they decode latents); the timeline figures state it.

Feature distillation: the verdict

Five Sonnet agents distilled the full FLUX.2 decoder's intermediate activations into small students (FitNets hints through 1×1 adapters, progressive stage-wise training, attention/Gram/relational losses, weight inheritance by slicing the full decoder, hints plus a late hinge/R1 GAN), each with an output-only ablation of the same student and steps. Re-measured on the 300 held-out 1024² images: the feature losses never beat output-only distillation on SSIM or LPIPS (5 of 5); only one improved ΔE (1.57 vs 1.94). Slicing the full decoder's channels destroyed it (SSIM 0.79) and the recovery was too short. Caveat: about an hour of training each; hint losses may pay off with far longer schedules (BK-SDM trains much longer) [FitNets; BK-SDM].
