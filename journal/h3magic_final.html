H3 Magic: final results

We stack adapters (LoRAs) on MiniMax H3, a text-to-video model, so that it draws a subject on a flat 50% gray background, as video and as images. The adapters are trained one on top of the other: Pexels clips (video look), image-gen (single frames), then gray magic (our MAGICK subjects on gray).

One thing went wrong and was fixed: our first adapters broke H3's built-in guidance (H3 has classifier-free guidance baked in, and plain training removed it, so outputs came out garbled). We retrained with a guidance loss. Every row on this page is a retrained one, generated at CFG 1. The full history is in the long journal page.

Glossary
H3
MiniMax H3, a text-to-video model (480p class). We use it at 512 × 512, 141 frames (5.875 s) at 24 fps, 30 steps. It can also make a single frame, which is an image.
Adapter / LoRA
A small set of extra weights (rank 16) trained on top of a big model. It is a weight difference Δ that gets added to the model.
Bake
Add an adapter into the model weights for good (weight 1), then train the next adapter on top. Each adapter here is trained on a model with all earlier adapters baked in.
Pexels
Pexels is a stock video site. The Pexels adapter is trained on 10,000 real Pexels clips (first 6 s, 512 × 512) with H3-style captions. It makes H3 produce Pexels-like video.
MAGICK
Our dataset of images of objects, with alpha mattes. Here we use its RGB composited on a 50% gray background, 512 × 512.
Gray magic
The adapter trained on 10,000 MAGICK images on 50% gray. The captions never mention the gray, so the adapter must learn it. The goal: a subject on a flat gray background.
CFG
Classifier-free guidance scale. CFG 1 means no extra guidance pass: the model runs once per step. H3 is built for CFG 1.
Guidance loss
A training loss (musubi-tuner's method) that keeps H3's baked-in guidance while training an adapter, so the adapter works at CFG 1.
Ablation
A control experiment with one thing changed. Here: one LoRA trained on gray MAGICK only, directly on stock H3, with no Pexels or image-gen stage before it.
