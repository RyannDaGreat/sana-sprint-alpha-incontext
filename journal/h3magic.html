H3 Magic: results, one row per stage

Same 20 prompts and fixed seeds in every row. Every item is the unmodified 512×512 output, labeled with its seed, seconds to generate and weight equation. Dataset pages: Pexels clips, gray magic images.

We build an RGB-on-gray version of the MiniMax H3 text-to-video model with adapters trained one on top of the other. Each row below is the model W = W_H3 + a·Δ_pexels + b·Δ_imagegen + c·Δ_graymagic. Rows appear as they are evaluated.

What went wrong with the first adapters, and the fix (2026-11-22)

The first adapters (rows marked CFG 1 without "v2") make H3 worse: garbled details, wobbly shapes, floating motion. Cause: the released H3 weights are CFG-distilled (guidance is baked in, so H3 runs without classifier-free guidance). We trained the LoRAs with the plain flow-matching loss, which undoes that distillation; H3's own fine-tuning docs (musubi-tuner) warn of exactly this. Measured: about 80% of the Pexels adapter's change points toward H3's unconditional prediction, and its quality falls steadily with the adapter weight.

Two fixes, both shown below: (1) CFG 2.5 at generation with the same adapters repairs them (rows marked "generated with CFG 2.5"; 2× the generation time). (2) Retraining the normal way: the ostris v3 training adapter is used only during training, learning rate 1e-4, then generation on plain H3 at CFG 1 (rows marked "v2").

Glossary
H3
MiniMax H3, a text-to-video model (480p class). We use it at 512 × 512, 141 frames (5.875 s) at 24 fps, 30 steps. It can also make a single frame, which is an image.
Adapter / LoRA
A small set of extra weights (rank 16) trained on top of a big model. It is a weight difference Δ that gets added to the model.
Bake
Add an adapter into the model weights for good (weight 1), then train the next adapter on top. Each adapter here is trained on a model with all earlier adapters baked in.
Δ notation
Δ_pexels, Δ_imagegen, Δ_graymagic are the weight differences of the three adapters.
Weight equation
How a model in a row is built at inference: W = W_H3 + a·Δ_pexels + b·Δ_imagegen + c·Δ_graymagic. Weight 0 turns an adapter off. The equation is printed under every item.
Pexels
Pexels is a stock video site. The Pexels adapter is trained on 10,000 real Pexels clips (first 6 s, 512 × 512) with H3-style captions. It makes H3 produce Pexels-like video.
MAGICK
Our dataset of images of objects, with alpha mattes. Here we use its RGB composited on a 50% gray background, 512 × 512.
Gray magic
The adapter trained on 10,000 MAGICK images on 50% gray. The captions never mention the gray, so the adapter must learn it. The goal: a subject on a flat gray background.
